A neonatal incubator whose every decision can be read.

Syncubator is a low-cost neonatal incubator. It holds warmth, humidity and oxygen at the levels a clinician chooses, watches the baby's environment and vital signs around the clock, and shows the same live picture on the incubator's own screen, a phone, or this website.

Why build another incubator

The infants who need one most are often where one is hardest to afford.

Physiology

Preterm infants lose heat faster than they can make it.

A large surface area for their mass, little fat and an immature thermoregulatory response mean that in an ordinary room a preterm or low-birth-weight infant cools quickly, and hypothermia in the first hours of life is strongly associated with mortality.

Cost

Certified incubators do this well — at a price many district hospitals cannot meet.

A warmed, humidified, oxygen-enriched microenvironment held within limits, without continuous manual attention, is exactly what commercial incubators provide. Their cost keeps them out of the wards that need them.

Upkeep

Built to be looked after where it is used.

Syncubator is designed so that a hospital's own staff can understand, adjust and repair it, without waiting on a distant manufacturer for every fault.

How it works

Three subsystems, one shared view.

Climate and oxygen control, alarms that keep watch whether or not anyone is looking, and a single application that reaches the incubator from wherever staff are.

Climate & oxygen

Steady warmth, humidity and oxygen.

The clinician sets the air temperature, humidity and oxygen level, and the incubator keeps them there on its own: warming, humidifying and adding oxygen as needed, so the environment settles gently instead of swinging up and down.

  • Clinician-setTargets are chosen and changed from any screen.
  • Hands-offNo constant manual adjustment; control can be switched off at any time.
  • RemembersSettings and alarm limits are kept through a power cut and restored when power returns.
Alarm system

Alarms that live on the incubator, not in the app.

Alarms are checked on the incubator itself and sound its own buzzer. If a browser closes, a phone sleeps or the Wi-Fi drops, the alarm still sounds. Every condition is watched continuously and follows medical alarm conventions rather than a plain threshold test.

  • 3 prioritiesHigh, medium and low, each with its own tone and banner colour.
  • 10 s onsetA breach must persist before it alarms, so a passing reading does not wake the ward.
  • LatchingAn alarm that clears on its own stays displayed until acknowledged. Silence lasts two minutes, then re-sounds.
Application

One application. The incubator's screen, a phone, this website.

The same application runs full-screen on the incubator's touchscreen, as an Android app, and here on the web behind a login. Every screen shows live readings, the camera and the same controls: targets, oxygen level, bed height, alarm acknowledgement and limits.

  • LiveEvery reading refreshes each second, over a secure connection.
  • LoginRemote access is restricted to approved e-mail addresses with a one-time code.
  • HonestIf a reading fails it is shown as a fault, never as a believable number.
Where it stands

Operating on the assembled unit; more bedside monitoring next.

Working now

  • Temperature, humidity and oxygen control from operator set points
  • Alarms with a short confirmation delay, latching, silence and a buzzer, tested on the incubator
  • Pulse oximetry: oxygen saturation and pulse rate on every screen, with their own alarms
  • Calibrated weight, oxygen and mattress-height readings
  • The incubator's touchscreen, an Android app and this web dashboard, all showing the same view
  • Settings that survive a power cut; recovers on its own after a restart

Next

  • Skin-temperature probes, and a mode that regulates warmth to the baby's own skin temperature
  • Accuracy check of the oxygen reading against a reference
  • Formal evaluation of alarm timing, control settling and weight accuracy
Team

Built at IIT Mandi.

Syncubator is developed by a student team at the Indian Institute of Technology Mandi, Kamand, Himachal Pradesh, with faculty mentorship. The alarm system, climate and oxygen control, and the application described on this page are the team's work for the current academic year; the enclosure, canopy and mattress platform form the shared platform on which they run.

Research prototype. Syncubator is an engineering project under development at IIT Mandi. It is not a certified medical device, has not been evaluated against IEC 60601 or any regulatory standard, and must not be used for the care of an infant.
